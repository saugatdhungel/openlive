How it works

This demo uses PeerJS for connection signaling and WebRTC for peer-to-peer video. It has no recording or built-in chat.

Screen sharing depends on browser/device support. Many mobile browsers do not allow a website to capture another app's screen; on those devices, use Android's supported screen-sharing flow or stream from a desktop browser. This demo is for small audiences: viewers connect peer-to-peer, so your upload bandwidth is shared among viewers. For reliable public streaming at scale, use a managed WebRTC/RTMP service and a backend.
